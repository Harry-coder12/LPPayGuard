FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? We usually reply within a day.

Contact us
Do I need an account?

No. There's no login, sign-up or cloud. PayGuard keeps everything on your phone.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Your records, photos and payslips never leave your phone. The internet is only used to handle the optional subscription.

Which countries are supported?

Rule packs cover US federal minimum wage and overtime (FLSA) and the UK National Minimum Wage. Elsewhere you still get a general arithmetic comparison.

How much does it cost?

Every feature is unlocked during a 14-day free trial with no card needed. After that, Premium is an optional subscription.

Can PayGuard recover my money?

No. It helps you understand and document a possible difference. It doesn't contact employers or file claims.
